How to Play Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe is a classic two-player game played on a 3x3 grid. Players take turns marking a square with their symbol (typically 'X' or 'O').

An empty 3x3 grid.

The goal is to get three of your symbols in a row, either horizontally, vertically, or diagonally, before your opponent does.

Player 'X' wins with three in a row.

If all nine squares are filled and neither player has three in a row, the game is a draw.

Click on any empty square to place your mark. Good luck!
